03 - PARTICIPATION REQUIREMENTS

Participants may submit either a model-based solution or an API-based
agent/workflow solution. To ensure a fair competition, the following
requirements apply:

MODEL-BASED SOLUTIONS
- Participants may train or fine-tune a model.
- Data augmentation, prompt rewriting, and additional external data are allowed.
- The submitted model must not exceed 70 billion total parameters (70B).

API-BASED AGENT / WORKFLOW SOLUTIONS
- All model API calls must be made through OpenRouter.
- Each model used in the workflow must not exceed 70 billion total parameters (70B).

Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) models: Eligibility is determined by the model's total
parameter count, not the number of parameters activated during inference.
