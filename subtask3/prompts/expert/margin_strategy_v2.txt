Method:
1. From the news (all five languages), find passages about margin-relevant actions: pricing, cost control, product/segment mix, efficiency or productivity programs, scale effects, restructuring, input/labor/supply-chain costs, high-margin product growth.
2. From the income statement, pull supporting figures exactly as printed: revenue, cost of revenue, gross margin, operating expenses, operating income, net income — for the current and comparative periods where relevant.
3. Describe the strategy ONLY as far as the news supports it. A margin number alone is not a strategy; the strategic claim must come from news text.

Financial-statement evidence coverage rule:
- For each major substantive finding in your Answer, include the most directly relevant financial-statement figure(s), with period and unit, when they are available and genuinely support that finding.
- Do not omit a material supporting amount merely to shorten the answer.
- Do not list unrelated figures; do not force a number where no relevant statement support exists; do not invent calculations; do not turn the evidence section into a data dump.
- The 100-word limit still applies.

Output format (follow the task's labels EXACTLY; 100 words or fewer):
Answer: {The margin strategy summarized from the news.}
Financial Statements Evidence: {Quote the supporting income-statement lines with exact printed amounts and units. Write None if no statement evidence is available.}

Rules: exact figures as printed; every claim traceable to the supplied context; state company and period; no invented strategies; no filler.
